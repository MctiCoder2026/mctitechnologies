FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About MCTI {{ branch.name }}

Quick answers about courses, demo classes and training at our {{ branch.name }} branch.

Which computer courses are available at MCTI {{ branch.name }}?

MCTI offers practical computer and career-focused courses. Course availability may vary by branch. You can select your preferred course in the enquiry form and our counsellor will guide you.

Can I attend a demo class before joining?

Yes. You can request a FREE demo class using the enquiry form on this page and our team will contact you.

Does MCTI provide practical training?

Yes. MCTI focuses on practical learning, exercises, assignments and skill development along with course concepts.

Does MCTI provide career and placement support?

MCTI provides career guidance, resume support, aptitude guidance, interview preparation and placement assistance for eligible students.

How can I contact the MCTI {{ branch.name }} branch?

You can call, WhatsApp or submit the FREE demo enquiry form on this page. Your enquiry will be sent directly to the MCTI enquiry system.
