MCTI One – Account & Data Deletion

MCTI One is provided by MCTI Technologies for students of Maharashtra Computer Training Institute.

Request Account Deletion

If you wish to request deletion of your MCTI One account and associated personal data, please contact MCTI Technologies using the details below.

Please include your Student ID, registered mobile number, and your request for account deletion so that we can verify your identity.

What Happens After Your Request?

After verification, we will process deletion or anonymisation of personal data associated with your MCTI One account where applicable.

Certain admission, academic, attendance, payment, receipt, accounting, or other records may be retained where required for legal, regulatory, academic, fraud-prevention, or legitimate record-keeping purposes.
