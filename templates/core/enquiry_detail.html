Standard Counselling Roadmap
  1. First call: understand goal, current skill, preferred timing and course.
  2. Send Welcome with course link and approved approx. fee.
  3. Record the response and agree on the next follow-up date.
  4. Follow-up 1: clarify questions and course suitability.
  5. Follow-up 2: arrange a centre visit or counselling appointment.
  6. Follow-up 3: discuss concerns and the next decision.
  7. Offer Aptitude/Scholarship assessment where relevant.
  8. Closing: confirm course, batch, final fee and admission steps.

Follow the agreed dates. Stop promotional follow-ups if the person declines. Record the actual outcome after each contact.
